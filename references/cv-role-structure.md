# CV Role Structure

Given the Role Type set by the employment coach, apply the corresponding structure below. Role Type is multi-select — combinations are common.

## Skills-based CV — when to use

A skills-based CV uses the categorized skills block format (SkillsHeading / Skills custom-style divs) placed prominently before `## EXPERIENCE`. Use it in these cases — regardless of Role Type:

- **Demand gen emphasis** — the JD foregrounds demand generation, pipeline ownership, or growth marketing as a primary function
- **Non-product-marketing roles** — strategy, evangelism, content strategy, marketing operations, growth, or any role where PMM is not the primary frame
- **VP roles of any kind** — the broader scope needs establishing upfront before experience

For {{USER_FIRST_NAME}}'s core roles (Head of Marketing, Head of PMM, Director PMM, Director Product Marketing, founding marketer) — a one-line pipe-separated competencies line is sufficient. The title communicates the domain; the bullets carry the proof. A prominent skills block adds no value and takes up space.

---

## Builder or Leader (or both)

- Frame {{USER_FIRST_NAME}} as a function-builder who led through acquisition.
- Emphasize strategic ownership, commercial outcomes, breadth of function owned as sole leader, leadership-team membership.
- Skills section: one pipe-separated competencies line in Normal style, no heading, placed between summary and `## EXPERIENCE`. Maximum 8 terms.
- No standalone Key Achievements section.

## Scaler

- Emphasize growth, team leadership, and scaling what's already working.
- Skills section: categorized block (3–4 category labels, pipe-separated terms). Maximum 4 lines. No bullet points or grid.
- A compact Key Achievements block (4–6 items, single column, `List Paragraph` style) is acceptable before `## EXPERIENCE` when the JD suggests front-loaded proof points.

## Specialist

- Emphasize craft depth and independent delivery.
- Lean into the specific domain the role requires, using the coach's Role emphasis as the anchor.
- Skills section: categorized block (3–4 category labels, pipe-separated terms). Maximum 4 lines. No bullet points or grid.
- A compact Key Achievements block (4–6 items, single column, `List Paragraph` style) is acceptable before `## EXPERIENCE` when the JD suggests front-loaded proof points.

## Combinations

Lead with the stronger signal for the specific JD. Builder + Scaler → Builder framing with scaling evidence in the proof points. Specialist + Leader → Specialist framing with leadership scope clearly established.

In all cases: frame the most recent role using the approved career-event framing from `candidate-rules.md` Section 7 — not as a job title alone.

## Tools section — when to include

Include `## TOOLS` for **Specialist** and **Builder** roles **only if the JD explicitly discusses tools, platforms, or specific technology** (e.g., mentions CRM, marketing automation, analytics platforms, documentation tools, or asks for tool proficiency).

- If the JD names tools: include `## TOOLS` with a selection from `candidate-rules.md` Section 8 that matches or is adjacent to what the JD calls out. Do not list everything — select the most relevant categories.
- If the JD does not mention tools: omit `## TOOLS` entirely for all role types.
- For **Leader** and **Scaler** roles: do not include `## TOOLS` regardless of JD content — leadership roles are evaluated on outcomes and scope, not tool proficiency.

## Leadership and mentorship credentials — always include for these roles

For all **leadership roles at any seniority level** and all **founding roles** (because founding roles can become leadership), always surface the following in the CV. Use in the ADDITIONAL section, skills section, or as a standalone line depending on space:

- **G-CMO Academic Mentor & Coordinator** — Advanced PMM Leadership Program: AI-Enhanced, G-CMO Academy, Cohort #3 (May 2026, Check Point). This is a faculty/mentor role — not a student credential. Use it to signal that peers in the industry trust {{USER_FIRST_NAME}} to develop senior PMM leaders.
- **Peer advisory contributions** — ongoing advisory sessions with marketing leadership peers.
- **Pro bono mentoring** — weekly mentoring of a junior founding marketer at a D2C company.
