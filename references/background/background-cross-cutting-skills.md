# Cross-Cutting Skills

Skills, capabilities, and qualifications that apply across multiple roles and companies — as opposed to company-specific role facts, which live in `background-role-facts-<company>.md`.

*What to put here: verb-backed capabilities you DO — not subject-matter areas you know ABOUT (that belongs in `03-framework.md` §Domain depth instead), and never a job title or role label. Technical skills, tools, certifications, languages, and cross-cutting achievements that are not tied to a single company. The pipeline's Skills section in the CV draws from this file, capped at 3 non-overlapping groups — see `writer-craft/SKILL.md` §5's three-way test before adding an item here.*
