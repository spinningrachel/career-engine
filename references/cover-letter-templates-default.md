# Generic Cover Letter Templates — Universal Architecture

Two templates split by psychological distance. Parameterized for any profession, role type, geography.

Layer 1 (universal mechanics) and Layer 2 (per-user parameters) embedded. Layer 3 (user-specific proof) to be filled in at composition.

---

## Shared Invariants (All Users)

| Element | Form |
|---|---|
| Greeting | `Hi to the {COMPANY} team!` Add `and to {REFERRER_NAME}` only for a real referrer. Never "the {NAME}". |
| Sign-off | `Looking forward to next steps,` or `Excited for what's ahead!` then {YOUR_NAME}. |
| Length | Max {MAX_WORDS} words (default 320). Aim for {MIN_WORDS}–{MAX_WORDS} (default 270–320). {SENTENCE_COUNT_MIN}–{SENTENCE_COUNT_MAX} sentences (default 12–22). |
| Sentence rhythm | Mean {MEAN_SENTENCE_LENGTH} words (default 14–23) with deliberate spread. Every letter carries one 2–5 word sentence and one {LONG_SENTENCE_LENGTH}+ word sentence (default 35–50). |
| Sentence engine | Subject-first active declarative. No questions unless deliberate (0–5 flex). No expletive openers ("There is/are"). No abstract label noun-phrase subjects. |
| Punctuation | Zero em dashes. Zero colons in body copy. Zero semicolons. Commas chain clauses and lists. Exclamations {EXCLAMATION_COUNT} per letter (default 1–2). Parenthetical asides for warmth, never hedging. |
| Vocabulary | Common words used variedly. Never upgrade to rarer synonym. Pick the right word and repeat it. |
| Person and tense | First-person dense. Second person sparse (0–2 per letter). Present-tense spine, past tense for proof stories. |
| Proof anchor | `At {COMPANY}, I {VERB}...` with comma. Name company, product, artifact specifically. Zero vague nouns. |
| Philosophy before proof | One belief paragraph precedes career evidence. |
| Employer's logic adopted | One passage argues the buyer's own market economics in their vocabulary, sourced from the JD's About/culture text. |
| Parallel lists | Real parallels of real things pass (including 4–5 items). Rhetorical tricolons built to impress are banned. Max one triad per letter. |
| Identity idiom | One per letter, rotated from {IDENTITY_IDIOM_POOL}. Default: `at heart` / `at my core` / `to the core`. Never two forms in one letter. |
| Voice | Active always. Confident, warm, direct, slightly informal. Credit collaborators. |

**Belief-formula variants (pick one):**
1. `I fundamentally believe {DOMAIN_CLAIM}.`
2. `I truly believe that {X}, {Y}, and {Z} go hand-in-hand.`
3. `It's my core belief that {CLAIM}.`
4. `I treat every {UNIT_OF_WORK} as a living cycle, so {CONSEQUENCE}.`

**Short reset variants (≤8 words, after a long chain):**
1. `I localized for {REGION} again at {COMPANY}.`
2. `{ACTIVITY} from scratch is what I love most.`
3. `{ATTRIBUTE} is the job, and {ATTRIBUTE} is what I am.`
4. `That's just the tip of what I'd bring.`

**Attribution-safe proof phrasings (mandatory forms):**
1. Founding role: `Founding {ROLE} at {COMPANY} as {COMPANY} grew {METRIC} from {START} to {END}.`
2. Leadership: `Led {SCOPE} across {N} {DISCIPLINES} during {CONTEXT} growth.`
3. Influence: `Influenced {METRIC} at {COMPANY}.` (never "generated" or "drove")
4. Scale: `Scaled {FUNCTION} to {N} {TEAM_MEMBERS}` or `through {OUTCOME}.`
5. Fractional: `Delivered {SCOPED_WORK} for {CLIENT}.` (never "owned {ROLE} at {CLIENT}" unless employed full-time)

**JD echo mechanic (standard across all letters):**
- Lift 1–2 token verbatim anchors from the JD
- Embed them in your own syntax
- Never reproduce a JD sentence at 10+ shared words
- Answer the culture/About text, every bonus line you honestly claim, and the JD's objection-shaped text
- Skip operational checklist bullets (timelines, specific tools, reporting structures, metrics definitions)

**Banned in all letters:**
- Em dashes, colons in body, semicolons
- AI-tell words: crucial, pivotal, showcase, robust, seamless, comprehensive, leverage (verb), foster, landscape, transformative, foundational, spearhead
- Hollow self-description: seasoned, passionate, results-driven, track record, thought leadership, experienced
- Destination-focused idioms (unless verbatim from your own input)
- Destiny formulas: "felt like you wrote it for me," "I knew this was mine," "meant for me" — state the reaction directly
- "I know how to sell/position X" without naming company and proof
- Pivot formula: "doesn't just X, it Y" / "It's not just X, it's Y"
- False range: "everything from X to Y"
- Approach announcements ("My approach is...") — show it instead
- Credibility tags: "Trust me," "to be honest"
- Personal destiny words from your own voice calibration (ask setup to identify these)

# TEMPLATE A — Cold / Scaffold

**Use when:** No referral, no personal tie, US/neutral-distance company, metrics-driven or developer reader.

**Dials:** {MAX_WORDS} words (default ≤320), {SENTENCE_COUNT_MIN}–{SENTENCE_COUNT_MAX} sentences (default 12–18), contractions light (default 0–1 per 100w), exclamations 0–1 beyond greeting, numerals {NUMERAL_COUNT_MIN}–{NUMERAL_COUNT_MAX} (default 2–8, for metrics readers).

## Block 1 — Opening (2–3 sentences)
Within the first two sentences the reader knows why this person, this company, right now. The context must be non-transferable.

**Variants:**
1. `Thanks to {REFERRER} for flagging the {ROLE} role. The timing is perfect, and I am a {SELF_LABEL} {IDENTITY_IDIOM}.`
2. `Reading the {COMPANY} posting, it was clear you're looking for a {SELF_LABEL}, which is why this role is so appealing.`
3. `As a {SELF_LABEL} who {SPECIFIC_PRODUCT_CLAIM}, I read the {ROLE} description and recognized my own work in it.`
4. `I just finished {SPECIFIC_RECENT_PROOF} at {COMPANY}, so the {ROLE} role reads like the natural next build.`

## Block 2 — Philosophy Paragraph (2–3 sentences)

**Variants:**
1. `I fundamentally believe {DOMAIN_CLAIM}. {MECHANISM}. {CONSEQUENCE with 1–2 JD tokens}.`
2. `{OUTCOME} is what I optimize for, and it is never a one-time win. {WHY}. {EMPLOYER_ECONOMICS consequence}.`
3. `I treat every {UNIT} as a living cycle. {PARTS}, and {RESULT}.`
4. `Most {BUYER_TYPE} expect {BEHAVIOR}, and winning their attention means balancing {X} with {Y} across {SURFACE}, {SURFACE}, and {SURFACE}.`

## Block 3 — Scaffold Transition + 3 Bullets

**Scaffold-line variants (period at end):**
1. `Here is how my background fits this strategy.`
2. `Here is how my experience maps to what you're building at {COMPANY}.`
3. `Three parts of my background do this exact work.`

**Exactly three bold-labeled bullets**, 2–3 sentences each. Bullet 1 = JD core competency. Bullet 2 = scale/leadership proof. Bullet 3 = differentiator.

**Bullet-body variants:**
1. `**{LABEL}.** I mix {TECHNICAL_DEPTH} with {BUSINESS_IMPACT} to {OUTCOME}. At {COMPANY}, I {PROOF + metric}.`
2. `**{LABEL}.** I've built {DISCIPLINE} from scratch repeatedly. I established the function at {COMPANY_1}, led {N} {TEAM_MEMBERS} during {GROWTH_CONTEXT}, and was the founding {ROLE} as {COMPANY_2} grew {METRIC_START} to {METRIC_END}.`
3. `**{LABEL}.** I {TECHNICAL_ACHIEVEMENT} at {COMPANY}, and I {BUSINESS_IMPACT_PROOF}. I equip {STAKEHOLDER} with {DELIVERABLE} that {OUTCOME}.`
4. `**{LABEL}.** I {INDEPENDENT_ACHIEVEMENT} with {QUALITY_PROOF}. I speak "{DOMAIN_LANGUAGE}" fluently and can message {JD_TECHNOLOGY} into {VALUE_PROPOSITION}.`
5. `**{LABEL}.** I scale {FUNCTION} using {TOOLS_AND_METHODS}, primarily {TOOL}, {TOOL}, and {TOOL}.`

## Block 4 — Objection Preemption (1–2 sentences)

**Mid-letter variants:**
1. `With a small team, I {SMALL_TEAM_PROOF}. {WORK_STYLE_FIT} have been my only way of working for {TIME_PERIOD}.`
2. `I have {TEAM_BUILDING_PROOF}, and I genuinely love it as much as the {FUNCTIONAL_WORK}.`
3. `I have deep familiarity with {COMPETITOR}, {COMPETITOR}, and others.`

**P.S. variants (for screening questions or ATS concerns):**
1. `p.s. Regarding the {SCREENING_QUESTION}, {CONCRETE_PROOF}!`
2. `p.s. On the {REQUIREMENT} question, {FACT}, so {CONSEQUENCE} is already part of my routine.`
3. `p.s. One note on {OBJECTION}. {REBUTTAL grounded in date or number}.`

## Block 5 — Close (1 sentence)
Vision teaser or diligence proof. No commitment vows in cold letters.

**Variants:**
1. `I look forward to discussing how I can help scale {COMPANY}.`
2. `I can't wait to learn the {COMPANY} vision directly from {DECISION_MAKER} and execute our agreed strategy.`
3. `I have {N} ideas for {JD_SCOPE} I want to explore with you.`

# TEMPLATE B — Warm / Woven

**Use when:** Referral, named individual, local market, personal product connection, trust-driven buyer, founding role, or strong culture fit.

**Dials:** {MAX_WORDS} words (default ≤320), {SENTENCE_COUNT_MIN}–{SENTENCE_COUNT_MAX} sentences (default 12–20), contractions on, exclamations 1–2 beyond greeting (never doubled), numerals {NUMERAL_COUNT_MIN}–{NUMERAL_COUNT_MAX} (default 0–2), second person {SECOND_PERSON_COUNT} (default 0–2), one parenthetical aside allowed.

**Required phrasing (founding/build-from-scratch roles only):** `{STRATEGIC_LABEL}` (default "strategic builder") appears exactly once in the letter. Once, not twice.

## Block 1 — Opening (2–3 sentences)

**Origin-story variants:**
1. `I'm writing about the {ROLE} role, which I saw when {REFERRER} posted it in {PLACE}.`
2. `When I saw the {ROLE} role, I wrote the same day.`
3. `When I saw that {COMPANY} is building {JD_SUBJECT} and looking for a founding {ROLE} to build the whole engine from zero, I applied immediately.`
4. `I have followed {COMPANY} for {TIME_PERIOD}, so seeing the {ROLE} role open was a genuinely good day.`

**Connection variants (state the reaction directly, no destiny formula):**
1. `I'm a {FUNCTIONAL_LEADER} and a {PRODUCT_CONNECTION} of {PRODUCT}.`
2. `I've been using {PRODUCT} since {TIME_ANCHOR}.`
3. `{BUYER_TYPE} prefer exactly the {COMMUNICATION_STYLE} language that is my natural register, and finding a role built around that is why I'm writing.`
4. `A {COMPANY_TYPE} heading toward {JD_DIRECTION} is exactly the build I want next.`

**Self-definition variants:**
1. `I'm a {STRATEGIC_LABEL} {IDENTITY_IDIOM}, and it's my core belief that {FOUNDING_PRINCIPLE}.`
2. `I'm a {DOMAIN_DESCRIPTOR} {IDENTITY_IDIOM}, and my natural go-to language is {COMMUNICATION_STYLE}.`
3. `{IDENTITY_FRAMING} I'm a {SELF_LABEL}, a {SELF_LABEL}, and a {SELF_LABEL} who {CAPABILITY}.`

## Block 2 — Philosophy Paragraph (2–4 sentences)
Contractions on. Demonstrative threading binds it to the opening.

**Variants:**
1. `{OUTCOME} is what I optimize for. {MARKET_REALITY}, so {CONSEQUENCE}. That's why I run {FUNCTION} as a cycle.`
2. `I truly believe that {X}, {Y}, and {Z} go hand-in-hand, and that's {WHY_THIS_ROLE}.`
3. `{BUYER_TYPE} make decisions based on {FACTOR}, {FACTOR}, and {FACTOR}, and this often requires {PROCESS_REALITY}.`
4. `Good {ROLE_PLURAL} want to {INTRINSIC_MOTIVATION}, and {COMPANY_ACTIVITY} is {EMOTIONAL_REACTION} for that reason. You take the work and adapt it to each {STAKEHOLDER}, learning the whole way through.`

## Block 3 — Woven Proof Paragraph 1 (3–4 sentences)
Locative anchors, prose chronology, credentials and dates for trust readers, anecdotes for consumer readers.

**Anchor variants:**
1. `At {COMPANY}, I led {SCOPE_LIST}, working shoulder to shoulder with {STAKEHOLDER_TYPE}.`
2. `At {COMPANY}, I localized {WORK} for {REGION} by {REGION}, working alongside the {SPECIALIST_TYPE} who knew each {CONTEXT}.`
3. `At {COMPANY}, I owned {SCOPE_LIST}.`
4. `I don't remember a time when I haven't had to move fast at work. {COMPANY_1}, {COMPANY_2}, {COMPANY_3}, {COMPANY_4}, all different sizes, all with dynamic needs.`
5. `{DOMAIN} I've been learning since {TIME_ANCHOR}, and I haven't stopped since, through {CONTEXT_1} and well beyond.`

Follow with one short reset variant.

## Block 4 — Woven Proof Paragraph 2 (2–4 sentences)

**Founding/differentiator variants:**
1. `As the founding {ROLE} at {COMPANY}, I owned {SCOPE_CATALOG}, end to end and through {OUTCOME_EVENT}.`
2. `I was the founding {ROLE} as {COMPANY} grew {METRIC_START} to {METRIC_END}, and {OUTCOME_EVENT}.`
3. `I've built a whole {FUNCTION} from zero before, alongside {SUPPORTING_WORK}.`
4. `At {COMPANY}, I spent the first {TIMEFRAME} {RESEARCH_ACTIVITY} before writing a line of {DELIVERABLE}, and every {UNIT} after that was built on what I learned.`
5. `At {COMPANY}, I ran {FUNCTION} on {RESOURCE_CONSTRAINT}, stretching {COST_LEVER} until {OUTCOME}.`

## Block 5 — Objection Preemption (1–2 sentences, last third)

**Variants:**
1. `This is important stuff, and perhaps a little {CONCERN} for a {BUYER} who {NEED} given their {PRESSURE} but also has to {DUTY}.`
2. `The {CONCERN} part doesn't scare me. I usually {PRACTICE_EVIDENCE}.`
3. `I'm flexible, and I know how it is to work in {ORG_TYPE_1} just like I know {ORG_TYPE_2}.`
4. `I'm a {SELF_LABEL} who wants to {JD_OFFER_PARAPHRASE} and do this work well for the long term.`

For role step-downs, frame as deliberate choice (autonomy, domain fit, stage, scope). One plain identity sentence is enough. Never protest twice.

## Block 6 — Close (1–2 sentences)
Commitment or desire statement. One exclamation maximum.

**Variants:**
1. `I'd love to own all {SCOPE} at {COMPANY}!`
2. `I can't wait to join {COMPANY} and contribute to its {DIRECTION} while continuing to grow myself.`
3. `I can't wait to talk about this role with you.`
4. `I have so many {DOMAIN} ideas I want to explore with you.`

## Pre-Send Checklist (Universal)

- [ ] Word count ≤ {MAX_WORDS}; one 2–5 word sentence and one {LONG_SENTENCE_LENGTH}+ word sentence present
- [ ] Zero em dashes, zero body colons, zero semicolons (find-and-replace the text)
- [ ] Banned-vocabulary scan clean per your personal voice calibration file
- [ ] Attribution-safe phrasings used exactly (no overstating, no vague nouns)
- [ ] Every claim traces to documented background or your own words; nothing repeats your CV
- [ ] Opener is non-transferable to any other company
- [ ] Philosophy precedes proof; max one triad; one identity idiom
- [ ] For founding/build-from-scratch: {STRATEGIC_LABEL} appears exactly once
- [ ] JD culture/About text answered; bonus lines answered where honest; operational bullets skipped
- [ ] No sentence shares 10+ consecutive words with the JD or any prior letter in the same market
- [ ] No block above {SIMILARITY_CEILING, default 0.75} BERT similarity with a same-market letter within {TIME_WINDOW, default 90 days}

## Setup Integration (For Plugin Use)

Run a mini forensic pass on the user's {ARCHIVE_SIZE, default 5–6} past cover letters to generate Layer 2 parameters:

1. **Fingerprint user's identity idioms** from {ARCHIVE} → populate {IDENTITY_IDIOM_POOL}
2. **Extract self-labels** → populate {SELF_LABEL} variants
3. **Detect voice tells** (em dashes, semicolons, pivots, destination words, credibility tags) → populate {BANNED_PER_USER}
4. **Measure word count, sentence length, contraction rate, exclamation placement** → populate dial ranges
5. **Extract company/role proof patterns** → populate attribution-safe phrases for their domain
6. **Resolve against career-data proof library** → validate that every {COMPANY}, {METRIC}, {OUTCOME} has a documented source

Users with no archive get corpus-neutral defaults:
- {IDENTITY_IDIOM_POOL} = `at heart` / `at my core` / `to the core`
- {MAX_WORDS} = 320
- {CONTRACTION_RATE} = 1–2 per 100w
- {BANNED_PER_USER} = universal AI-tells only
- {STRATEGIC_LABEL} = "strategic builder"

Regenerate {IDENTITY_IDIOM_POOL}, {SELF_LABEL}, {PROOF_PATTERNS} every {REGEN_INTERVAL, default 6 letters} as new delivered letters accumulate.
