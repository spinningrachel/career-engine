# How to use this framework

**Operating principle — the framework is the inventory; every outbound artifact is a curated subset.** This document holds your full positioning surface: every vertical, persona, proof point, and messaging frame that's true about you. No single CV, cover letter, recruiter call, or board conversation should present all of it. For each opportunity, pull only the verticals, proof points, personas, and messaging frames that fit the role, company stage, domain, and hiring persona in front of you. The framework is the library. Each artifact is a selection.

- **CVs and cover letters:** Pull commercial outcomes from §Proof points bank and verticals from §Domain depth that match the target role's domain and stage. Use the messaging frame from §Messaging that matches the hiring persona. Apply anti-positioning rules from §Anti-positioning.
- **LinkedIn About and headline:** Use a tagline from §Tagline and one-liner options + the 60-second elevator from §Elevator pitches.
- **Recruiter calls:** Open with the 15- or 60-second pitch (§Elevator pitches). Close with the ICP and search criteria (§Ideal target opportunities).
- **Board / analyst conversations:** Lead with category framing (§Category and market frame) and the core positioning statement (§Core positioning statement). Land proof points from §Proof points bank.
